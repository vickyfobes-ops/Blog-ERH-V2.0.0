# Elite Range Hood brand facts and editorial guardrails

## Brand naming

- The public brand name is always `Elite Range Hood`.
- Never use the standalone abbreviation `ERH` in customer-facing English or Chinese copy, SEO fields, excerpts, CTAs, anchor text, image alt text, captions, Word review documents, or Shopify article fields.
- The abbreviation may remain only in the established Skill/repository name, internal filenames, internal audit IDs, and technical schema identifiers.
- Treat any customer-facing occurrence of standalone `ERH` as a blocking validation error.

## Source priority

Resolve conflicts in this order:

1. Facts the user confirms in the current task.
2. Current Elite Range Hood product pages, installation manuals, care instructions, warranty, and policy pages.
3. Approved Elite Range Hood internal material.
4. Government, standards organizations, peer-reviewed research, and official manufacturer documentation.
5. Older Elite Range Hood articles.
6. Competitor articles only for discovering questions, structure patterns, and gaps.

## Brand and audience

Elite Range Hood sells custom handcrafted metal range hoods for the U.S. English market. Public product language currently includes copper, stainless steel, brass, and zinc. Current pages also discuss wall-mount, island, and under-cabinet contexts, but verify the exact product's supported installation and configuration before claiming availability.

Write primarily for high-intent homeowners, interior and kitchen designers, architects, contractors, installers, and trade partners. Present the hood as a coordinated system whose ventilation, capture area, mounting conditions, proportions, exterior form, finish, and installation must work together.

Use natural professional American English. Be confident but not breathless. Prefer concrete design and planning guidance over generic luxury language.

## Product-claim rules

- Never invent sheet thickness, metal grade, CFM, sone/noise rating, price, lead time, warranty, certification, origin, mounting method, or care procedure.
- Never transfer a competitor's option, dimension, material, liner, or installation practice to Elite Range Hood.
- Treat size, clearance, CFM, BTU, duct diameter, makeup air, gas ventilation, electrical work, structural support, and code as product- and jurisdiction-dependent.
- Prefer the exact cooking-appliance and liner instructions plus locally adopted code. Recommend the appropriate licensed HVAC, electrical, or contracting professional when needed.
- Do not equate general bare-copper antimicrobial research with a health or sanitizing claim for an Elite Range Hood product. Coatings, patina, time, environment, and test conditions matter.
- Before giving copper-care advice, distinguish natural/live, patinated, waxed, lacquered, and sealed finishes. Acids, abrasives, and oxidizers may change or damage a finish.
- Reverify price, availability, promotions, product count, policy, warranty, shipping, and contact information immediately before publication.

## Content clusters

Assign one primary cluster and no more than two supporting clusters:

1. Sizing, Ventilation & Installation
2. Materials, Finishes & Craftsmanship
3. Kitchen Style & Inspiration
4. Buying & Customization
5. Care & Ownership
6. Projects, Proof & Brand
7. Adjacent Rooms & Products, only when consistent with the current Elite Range Hood catalog and site strategy

Use one funnel label: Awareness, Consideration, Decision, or Ownership. Define one primary search intent and one primary conversion action.

## Minimum publication fields

- working title and final H1;
- primary keyword, entities, intent, cluster, and funnel stage;
- SEO title, normally 45–60 characters;
- meta description, normally 140–160 characters;
- short stable lowercase handle;
- excerpt distinct from the meta description;
- featured-image brief and alt text;
- verified outgoing internal-link plan;
- linked English Word document and linked Shopify HTML generated from the same source;
- two to five reverse-link tasks when practical;
- source log and fact notes;
- clean Markdown or Shopify-safe HTML, as requested.

## Publication boundary

Default to local drafts. Do not connect to or write to Shopify without explicit authorization for the exact online action. A request to write a blog, make it publication-ready, or produce Shopify HTML is not authorization to publish.

Verified contextual internal links belong in the local review package. Their presence does not authorize a Shopify write.
